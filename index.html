What we're looking for

Original work only. Articles on any subject approached with rigor and curiosity, poetry in any form, and short fiction that hasn't appeared anywhere else. We read across science, history, literature, philosophy, and art, and we're just as interested in the space between disciplines as within them.

Each issue includes

  • 1 article
  • 2 to 4 poems
  • 1 short story

We publish 4 issues a year, on a rolling schedule, we'll announce dates as we settle into a rhythm.

Length guidelines

  • Articles: 1,200 to 2,500 words
  • Short fiction: 1,500 to 3,500 words
  • Poetry: no strict limit, but keep it under roughly 60 lines

How to submit

  • Articles: one per submission.
  • Poetry: up to 10 poems in a single submission.
  • Short fiction: one story per submission.

Send your work as a plain document (.docx or .pdf) to the email above. Include a short author bio, 2 to 3 sentences, third person, telling readers a little about who you are. We'll publish this alongside your work.

Response time

You'll receive an acknowledgment of receipt within 7 days. Full editorial decisions may take longer, we're a small team reading carefully, not a mass operation.

Rights

You retain full copyright of your work. Publication with Fieldnotic is non-exclusive, you're free to republish your work anywhere else, before or after it appears with us.

A note on AI

Fieldnotic is a space for human voices and human thought. We're not interested in AI-generated writing, no matter how polished it might be. We want your sentences, your instincts, your particular way of seeing things, and we'll always prefer something a little rough and real over something smooth and synthetic.

Questions before submitting? Write to us at fieldnoticmagazine@gmail.com
