---
title: On Reading Slowly in a Fast Time
author: Us
---
There is a particular kind of attention that only slow reading gives back to a person  the sense of a sentence being turned over, considered, allowed to sit before the next one arrives. Most of what we read today asks nothing of us but recognition. It wants to be scanned, absorbed, forgotten. But the old writers   the ones worth returning to  wrote as if the reader had time. As if understanding were not a race but a walk.
Maybe that's what a journal like this one is really for: not to add more noise to an already loud world, but to make room, occasionally, for someone to think slowly in public, and for someone else to read slowly in return.
