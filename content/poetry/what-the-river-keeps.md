The river does not remember

the way we do, in pieces,

in aching returns to the same bend

of a single afternoon.

It simply keeps moving,

carrying whatever falls into it

a little further than before,

never once looking back

to see what it has lost.

Maybe that is its only mercy,

or its only failure.

I have never been able to decide

which one grief actually wants.
